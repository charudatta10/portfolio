### **🖋️ Handwritten Devanagari Script Recognition (2015)**

- **Objective**: Build machine learning models to recognize handwritten characters in Devanagari script.
- **Features**: Provided pre-processed datasets, training scripts, and evaluation tools.
- **Outcome**: Enabled researchers and developers to explore character recognition efficiently.
- **Academic Context**: Bachelor's Project, GEC Goa.

### **📡 Total Electron Content (TEC) as a Seismic Precursor (2017)**

- **Objective**: Use TEC data and Multi-Fractal Detrended Fluctuation Analysis (MFDFA) to identify seismic activities.
- **Key Insights**: Analyzed fractal patterns in ionospheric variations for early earthquake detection.
- **Academic Context**: Master's Project, GEC Goa & IIT Bombay.

---

### **🔄 Hybrid Evolutionary Optimization (HEO) of GANs (2019)**

- **Objective**: Enhance GAN training using evolutionary algorithms.
- **Key Contributions**:
  - **HEO**: Combined genetic algorithms with backpropagation for improved performance.
  - **Hybrid GAN**: Leveraged MFDFA in the discriminator to generate images faster and more efficiently.
  - **2D MFDFA**: Integrated multifractal analysis for temporal and spatial data, refining GAN learning.
- **Outcome**: Improved convergence and high-quality image generation.
- **Academic Context**: PhD Project, NIT Goa.
